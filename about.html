About Lead Wave

Discover our commitment to providing seamless, transparent, and reliable delivery services across Freetown and Sierra Leone.

Connecting People & Businesses with Purpose

Lead Wave (SL) Limited is a registered logistics company based in Freetown, Sierra Leone. Founded with a clear mandate to solve complex urban transport and package dispatch challenges, we deliver prompt, safe, and trackable courier solutions.

Whether bridging the gap between local e-commerce vendors and their customers or handling daily corporate document routing, our operations are optimized to guarantee reliability every step of the way.

Our Slogan

"Built for people. Driven by purpose."

This core philosophy guides our rider fleet, operational management, and customer care every single day.

Our Mission

To deliver excellence through customer-centric express logistics, leveraging efficient routing, dependable personnel, and modern tracking technology to simplify delivery for every Sierra Leonean enterprise and individual.

Our Vision

To become Sierra Leone’s most trusted and accessible last-mile logistics and dispatch service network, empowering commerce and community connectivity across the nation.

What Drives Us

Reliability & Safety

We treat every package with utmost care, ensuring packages reach their destination in original condition and strictly on schedule.

Customer Centricity

Our operational decisions center on delivering a seamless experience, transparent communication, and responsive support.

Speed & Accountability

With structured tracking systems and committed riders, we prioritize prompt last-mile delivery with verifiable proof of receipt.
